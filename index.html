Why stone never goes out of style

In the quarry above Estremoz the workers still split blocks by hand. They say marble tells you where it wants to break. The architect beside me nodded as though this were ordinary news, then explained why her new library will use nothing else.

What a coral reef sounds like

Marine biologist Ana Ruiz lowers a hydrophone over the side. At first there is only the hum of the boat. Then comes a crackle like frying oil: thousands of shrimp, all snapping at once, telling her the reef is alive.
